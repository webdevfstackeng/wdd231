AgriCommerce & Digital Trade Chamber (GADTC)

About Gaborone AgriCommerce & Digital Trade Chamber

The purpose of Gaborone AgriCommerce & Digital Trade Chamber is to connect agricultural producers, traders, transport providers, and digital payment service providers in Gaborone and surrounding areas while promoting local business growth and economic development.
