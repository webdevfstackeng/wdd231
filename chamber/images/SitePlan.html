Site Name

Gaborone AgriCommerce & Digital Trade Chamber (GADTC)

 

Site Purpose

The Gaborone AgriCommerce & Digital Trade Chamber (GADTC) is a local business association that connects agricultural producers, meat suppliers, transport providers, retailers, and digital payment service providers in Gaborone and surrounding areas. The website will provide business networking opportunities, a member directory, a marketplace showcase, industry news, events, and membership information to support local economic growth.

Target Market

· Supermarkets and grocery stores

· Local families

· Catering businesses

· Hotels and guest houses

· Hawkers and street vendors

· Fresh produce farmers

· Meat suppliers and butcheries

· Transport and logistics providers

· Digital payment agents and fintech businesses

Site Goals

· Provide a professional online presence for the chamber.

· Increase visibility of member businesses.

· Promote local agricultural and digital trade opportunities.

· Allow visitors to browse a searchable member directory.

· Encourage businesses to apply for chamber membership.

· Share upcoming events, workshops, and networking opportunities.

· Educate the public about digital payment services and local commerce initiatives.

 

User Personas

1. Small Business Owner – Sammy

Age: 29

Occupation: Hawker

Goals:  Looking for ideas to grow his business and also for upcoming coming events to collaborate with other resellers.

Needs from the site: Business directory, and events calendar.

 

 2. Produce Buyer – Naledi

Age: 42

Occupation: Catering business owner

Goals: Find reliable suppliers of fresh produce and meat.

Needs from the site: Marketplace listings and supplier contact details.

 

3. The New Resident: Rogers is contemplating starting a small fruits and vegetables shop. He is searching for business information regarding reliability of fresh produce supplies and farmers directory.

 

User Scenarios

· A local business owner wants to join the chamber and looks for membership benefits, fees, and the application form.

· A visitor considering relocation explores the business directory and local economic opportunities.

· A caterer searches for tomato and beef suppliers for an upcoming event.

· A digital payment agent wants to advertise their services through the chamber directory.

· A farmer checks the events page for the next agricultural networking workshop.

 

SEO

· Descriptive page titles and meta descriptions.

· All images with alt text.

· Heading hierarchy (H1, H2, H3).

· Site Submitted to Google Search Console.

· Connect Google Analytics.

· Encourage member businesses to link back to the chamber website.
